The looseness of the overall fabric (a sparse, far-flung network) is consistent with a work that reaches outward to many topics rather than compressing toward a single Rambanian core. *(Interpretation: the low alignment combined with high concept count is the clearest single signal of "braiding" here.)*

The strong presence of (דרך האמת) among the core concepts confirms that the "way of truth" — the kabbalistic register — is a structural pillar of the work, not an occasional aside.

**Sources and attribution.** The source base is broad (198 distinct citations) but shallow per-source, the typical profile of a wide-ranging author drawing on many authorities lightly. Scripture itself — cited as (הכתוב), (פסוק), and (תורה) — anchors the work, but alongside it stand the rabbinic authorities (רז"ל) and (חז"ל) and, tellingly, the first-person tradition-marker (קבלתי, "I have received"). The recurrence of (הרב) as both a core and a bridging concept suggests that an authoritative teacher-figure functions as a structural hinge linking discrete passages. *(Interpretation: that (תפארת) and (רחמים) are simultaneously the most recurrent and the most connective concepts indicates the work is organised around a single sefirotic equation rather than a diffuse set of themes.)*

**Characteristic equation.** The signature link of the whole commentary is the identification of mercy with the sefirah Tif'eret: (רחמים) "is called" (תפארת), "corresponds to" it, and "has it as a part," each attested strongly (18 occurrences apiece), with a softer "related to" relation reinforcing the same pairing. To this is joined the symbolic reading of the cherubim — (כרובים) "symbolizes" (תפארת) (13). This recurring cluster is the author's interpretive fingerprint: a stable, repeatedly redeployed mapping of the attribute of mercy and its cultic emblems onto Tif'eret.

**Sources and authorities.** The single most-cited work is the Kabbalistic *Sefer ha-Bahir* (ספר הבהיר:8), reinforcing the esoteric tilt signalled by (רמז). Rabbinic tradition is invoked through a cluster of attribution formulae — רז"ל:7, מאמרם:6, רבותינו:4 — alongside Midrash proper (מדרש:6; המדרש:5) and direct scriptural citation (הכתוב:5). Beyond the Torah, the commentator reaches into the Prophets and Writings — Isaiah (12), Job (7), Psalms (6), and Kohelet (5) — indicating a homiletic willingness to braid wisdom and prophetic material into the discussion. ### Ramban (Nachmanides) — Corpus Profile

**Status as anchor.** This profile describes the corpus against which all other commentators are aligned; the reported signature similarity of 1.0 is therefore a tautology of self-identity rather than a finding. Consequently the *braider / distiller* axis — designed to measure how a later author behaves around Ramban — cannot strictly be applied to Ramban himself. What can be characterised is his own authorial disposition, which in absolute terms is markedly **additive and expansive**: a very large concept inventory (942 concepts across 37 sections) held together by comparatively few connective threads (968 links, density 0.0022) and fragmenting into many small thematic clusters (223 communities). The reading: Ramban roams widely, opening many distinct interpretive nuclei rather than knotting everything to a few dense hubs — a "braider's" temperament in absolute profile, if not in the comparative sense.

**Method signature.** The relational fabric leans heavily on generic gestures of textual presence and citation — locating a concept in a verse (appearsIn, 734) and noting that the text speaks of something (mentions, 344) — with a moderate generic fraction (0.262). More interpretively committed relations follow: causal explanation (causes, 117), part–whole analysis (hasPart, 106), and naming/identification (isCalled, 104). The named exegetical registers are present but deliberately sparing: the hint (רמז, 5 occurrences) leads, followed by an even distribution across the plain sense (דרך הפשט, 2), the way of truth (דרך האמת, 2), the secret of the midrash (סוד המדרש, 2), the explanation of the verse (פירוש הכתוב, 2), and the parable (משל, 2). *Interpretation:* this thin but balanced spread of labels is consistent with Ramban's well-known layered hermeneutic, in which the esoteric way of truth (דרך האמת) and the hint (רמז) are invoked as signposts rather than worked through verbosely — the bulk of the prose proceeding under unlabelled plain explanation.

### §32 — The Shema and the Kabbalah of Unification (CONVERGENCE)

#### 1. The Shared "Green" Core
